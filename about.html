Our Mission

Citizens lack a streamlined, hyper-local platform to report infrastructure failures in real-time. CivicTracker bridges the gap between citizens and local authorities by digitizing the reporting process for issues like potholes and broken streetlights, ensuring faster resolution and community accountability.
